A little about me

Hi, I'm Katy!

I'm a cartoonist, illustrator, stop-motion, and digital media artist in the San Francisco Bay Area. I'm currently studying Digital Media Art at San Jose State University.

Cartoons, 90s nostalgia, and video games inspire my bold & colorful artstyle. I'm also the artist behind the brand SparkToons. You can find my work online and catch me selling my art at local fan conventions.
